# Mezze

> Mezze is one local daemon and one command, `zm`, for a person or a small team with more coding agents than they can keep in their head. It carries mail between agents and people, work items whose claims really expire, and decisions a person approves from a phone, across Claude Code and Codex on every machine they run. Its licence is Apache-2.0; its source is not public yet.

Mezze is the agent and team layer on Potluck (https://potluck.sh), and works on one machine with no Potluck pool. It runs on macOS and Linux. On one machine it needs no account, no cloud and no network.
